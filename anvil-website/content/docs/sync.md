# Sync & Mesh

Sync & Mesh is the optional account layer for Anvil Desktop: end-to-end
encrypted sync across your devices, job execution on your own machines, and a
backend you choose — Anvil-hosted, your Cloudflare account, or any conformant
implementation of the frozen contract. Local-only remains the default; nothing
leaves a device until you pin a backend and sign in.
