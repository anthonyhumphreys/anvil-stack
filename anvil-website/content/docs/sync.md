# Sync & Mesh

Sync & Mesh is the optional account layer for Anvil Desktop: end-to-end
encrypted sync across your devices, job execution on your own machines, and a
backend you choose, including Anvil-hosted, your Cloudflare account, or any
conformant implementation of the frozen contract. Sync & Mesh stay free
permanently. Local-only remains the default; nothing leaves a device until you
pin a backend and sign in. Authentication, lifecycle, security, and fair-use
rules still apply.
