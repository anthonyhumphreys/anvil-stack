# Sync & Mesh

Sync & Mesh is the optional account layer for Anvil Desktop. Sync keeps your workspaces, templates, custom agents and chosen settings in step across your machines, encrypted on the device before anything leaves it. Mesh lets one of your machines send work to another. You choose where the backend runs: Anvil-hosted, your own Cloudflare account, or any compatible server.

Every workspace starts local. Sync sends no workspace data to a backend until you choose one, sign in and enable Sync for that workspace. Anvil-hosted writes require a paid entitlement or a redeemed beta code. Checkout is closed and production hosting is not yet available; invited testers use time-limited codes without a payment method in the test environment. Running your own backend carries no Anvil subscription charge. See [pricing](/pricing).
